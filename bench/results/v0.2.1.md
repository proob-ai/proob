# Benchmark results: proob v0.2.1

The bug sets were not rerun for v0.2.1. The latest full measurement is still [v0.1.0-rc1](v0.1.0-rc1.md) (18 of 20 held-out trials, 9 of 10 post-cutoff bugs, no wrong `Holds` in 260 scored verdicts); read its numbers as v0.1.0's, not this release's.

v0.2.1 ships one fix, listed under v0.2.1 in [CHANGELOG.md](../../CHANGELOG.md): `proob shadow open` (and so `/proob:try`) stopped in any repository whose `.gitignore` ignores `.claude/`.

## What was verified instead

| Change since v0.2.0 | How it was verified |
|---|---|
| `proob shadow open` in a repository that ignores `.claude/`, `.claude/settings.json`, or `.gitignore` | A new integration test over the three ignore rules, in CI; locally, the fixed binary on a repository that ignores `.claude/`. |

## Not yet true

- The bug sets have not been measured on this release, including the effect of reserving `Holds` for proofs and models.
- No team outside this repository has used proob yet.
